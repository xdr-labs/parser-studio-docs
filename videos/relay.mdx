## Follow the steps

1. Enter **Manufacturer: Fortinet**, **Product: FortiGate**. Paste both received relay messages shown above.
2. Expand **Optional: declare Syslog forwarder hostnames**, enter **relay01**, leave **Maximum declared hops** at **1** and check the relay source preview: **2 of 2 recognized, 0 unsafe, 0 not matching**.
3. Click **Apply declared relay profile**, then **Analyze Logs**. The actual UI returned **2 of 2 log events parsed completely**.
4. Click **Review Fields** and inspect `srcip`, `dstip`, `action` and `sessionid` against the original vendor message. Continue to **Map Fields** and review Stellar Cyber 7.0 suggestions.
5. Continue to **Create Parser** and run **Run Final Test & Create Files**. The actual recording reached and passed this local final test. This does not imply native Fluentd or Stellar Cyber target verification.
